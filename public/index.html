iPhone & iPad (Safari)
Recommended for iOS

Apple iOS prevents opening downloaded HTML files in Safari directly. Instead, Add to Home Screen to run TradeCalc like a native iOS app with full offline capabilities:

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button (square with upward arrow ⎋ at bottom).
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (plus icon ⊞).
  3. Tap "Add" in top-right corner.
  4. Success: Opens fullscreen from your home screen, zero server delay, saves accounts, and works 100% offline!
How to Install on Android / PC / Mac (Chrome)
  1. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  2. Tap "Install TradeCalc" or "Add to Home screen".
Direct Standalone Web File (.html)

Download the entire TradeCalc calculator as a single HTML file for PC, Mac, or Android (double-click to open anytime in Chrome, Edge, or Safari on Mac).
